What Signal Base does

Sports betting models that show their work. Every pick comes with its price and its result, win or lose.

1

Daily model picks

See today’s picks for each sport and bet type, with the best price across sportsbooks.

2

A public record

Every pick is tracked after the game. Wins, losses and units won stay on the record, including the bad days.

3

Build your own model

Pick the bet types you care about, from moneylines to player props. Set your rules and see how many units they would have won.

# Support

Help for the Signal Base app and signalbase-ai.com.

## Contact

Email Signalbaseskinny@gmail.com for help with the app, your account, sign-in, or a subscription. That is the same address on the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy.

## Delete your account

In the app, open **Settings** and choose **Delete account**. That control is shown when you are signed in. When you delete your account in the app, it is removed right away, and remaining server data and backups are purged within 30 days.

If you cannot use that control, email Signalbaseskinny@gmail.com from the address on the account and ask us to delete it.

**What was wrong.** The pick screen's line-movement card read the OLDEST 50
snapshots of a market at the pick's book and called the last one "now". It
drives the header ("locked → now"), the verdict and the table. On any market
with more than 50 snapshots, "now" was old, often from before the pick. Props
had a second problem: about half of recent `player_prop_odds` rows are stamped
in Eastern time ("-04:00"), so ordering by the stamp as text is hours wrong
where offsets mix, and the oldest 50 could include rows from after the start.

**What changed.**

- Game lines (`fetchOddsHistory`). Every stamp in `odds` is UTC (measured), so
  the server cuts the window. One read takes the last pre-game snapshot before
  the pick's second; a second read takes the newest 50 from that second up to
  the start, in-play rows excluded. Both bounds are 19-character UTC text
  (`utcSecond`), which sorts correctly against all three stamp shapes in the
  table.
- Props (`fetchPropOddsHistory`). Never ordered or filtered by `snapshot_at` on
  the server. The whole series comes back (newest written first, capped at
  500; the longest series for the card's markets was 174 rows in the
  read-only pass) and is sorted on the phone by the parsed instant
  (`byTime`).
- `lib/lineHistory.sincePick` cuts both on the phone: the last snapshot at or
  before the pick (the pick is scored from a snapshot seconds to about 45
  minutes earlier, so a window starting AT the pick would hide most NHL
  props), then everything up to the start, which is the earlier of

**Session summary (2026-10-09 — MLB, WNBA, NBA and college football prop odds are stamped in UTC):**

mike: "fix the prop writers to stamp UTC too". Branch `claude/prop-odds-utc`,
from origin/master `3e65c630`. Two writer lines changed. No schema change, and

**What was wrong.** Two of the prop odds writers stamped each row in Eastern
time (`datetime.now(_ET).isoformat()`, ending "-04:00"): the shared MLB, WNBA
and NBA writer (`prop_odds_ingestor.py`) and the college football writer
(`ncaaf_prop_odds_ingestor.py`). The NFL and NHL writers stamp UTC. Measured
today, rows for games on 2026-10-09, counted by sport and by the last six
characters of the stamp: WNBA 161,572 and college football 2,414, all
"-04:00"; NHL 1,005, all "+00:00". For games on 2026-10-05 to 10-10,
1,990,335 rows end "-04:00" and 552,049 end "+00:00" (78% Eastern).

So `player_prop_odds.snapshot_at` held two offsets. Code that orders that
column as text inside one prop was right only because each prop's rows came
from one writer. That includes the database trigger that keeps each prop's
latest price, the odds pruner and the app's prop history. It would have broken
on 2026-11-01. The Eastern stamps would have turned "-05:00", and the 1am hour
that happens twice that night sorts backwards as text: "01:30-04:00" sorts
after "01:10-05:00", which is forty minutes later. The latest-price trigger
would have kept the older price.

**What changed.**

- Both writers now stamp `datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat()`, the same
  as the NFL and NHL writers. The slate date ("today", and each game's date)
  stays Eastern, so a pass at 11:30pm Eastern still asks for tonight's games.
- The stamp ends "+00:00", never "Z". The paid MLB backfill's resume check
  (`backfilled_dates`) treats a Pinnacle row ending "Z" as one it already
  bought, so a "Z" on live rows would make a re-run skip dates it should buy.
- The two notes that said the live writer stamps Eastern time were updated
  (the `backfilled_dates` docstring and its test's docstring). Their checks
  did not change.

**Readers.** Three separate sweeps of every reader and writer (code, database
objects, writers) found nothing that breaks. Every reader either converts the
stamp to a real time before comparing, or compares text only inside one prop
at one book. On switch day a prop's history is Eastern rows, then UTC rows,
and a UTC stamp always sorts after every earlier Eastern stamp: the UTC clock
reads 4 hours ahead for the same moment. Checked on the production database
under its own sort rules (ICU, en-US), not assumed from byte order: 618 pairs
across a full day, each an Eastern stamp followed by a UTC stamp 1 microsecond,
10 minutes or 6 hours later, all in time order, with and without fractional
seconds. The same check shows the old fall-back case out of order and the UTC
one in order. A read-only listing of every view and function that mentions the
prop odds tables found the same two views and four functions the database
sweep did, and no materialized view.

- One output changes: the latest-prop view the app reads

  refreshed after the deploy show "+00:00" and older ones keep "-04:00". The
  moment is the same; only the written offset differs. No app code reads that
  field. A search of `mobile/src` finds every use of `snapshot_at` on game
  odds, live game state or the prop history rows, and none on the
  latest-prop rows.
- The system health freshness check for prop odds takes the newest stamp as
  text across all sports. With two offsets it could pick a UTC row that is up
  to 4 hours older than the newest Eastern row. With every new row in UTC, it
  now reads the true newest.

corrects itself.

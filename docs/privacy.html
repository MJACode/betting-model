Signalbase Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-20

Signalbase is a personal sports analytics app that displays the output of a statistical model. This policy explains what data the app collects, what it does not collect, and how to contact us with questions.

What we collect

Nothing personal. Signalbase does not require an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not contain advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

What stays on your device

  • Your chosen bankroll amount (a single number you enter in Settings).
  • "Placed bet" flags you toggle on individual picks.

Both are stored locally using the operating system's standard on-device storage (AsyncStorage). They never leave your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

What the app reads from our backend

Signalbase reads publicly available model output (game and player projections, historical box-score statistics, model accuracy metrics) from our Supabase database. These reads are anonymous and read-only — the app cannot write to the backend, and the backend stores no information about you, your device, or your activity within the app.

Diagnostics

Apple's TestFlight and the App Store automatically collect basic crash reports and aggregate usage statistics if you have opted in to share them with developers in your iPhone settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers). We use these reports solely to fix crashes. We do not combine them with any other data.

Children

Signalbase is not directed at children under 17. The App Store age rating reflects sports-betting-related references in the displayed content.

What Signalbase is not

  • It is not a sportsbook. You cannot place a real-money wager from inside the app.
  • It is not investment or gambling advice. Model output is provided for research and educational purposes only.
  • It does not transmit your location.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

# Horse racing leads: market structure, models, commission, price paths

**Reading conventions**
- Every source cell gives the date read (all 2026-11-15) and the depth: **full** (whole page, or the parts cited), **abstract** (landing-page abstract only), **snippet** (search-engine excerpt only; page not opened), **not opened**.
- No PDFs were fetched, because of the no-download rule. Any figure that exists only in a PDF is marked unverified.
- ROI and POT (profit on turnover) are % of stake.
- "MBR" is Betfair's Market Base Rate: the commission charged on net winnings in each market.

**Column names are placeholders.** Only `BEST_AVAIL_BACK_AT_SCHEDULED_OFF` and the `WIN_PREPLAY_*` prefix came from our brief. Names like `WIN_BSP`, `PLACE_BSP`, `BACK_MARKET_PERCENTAGE` and `WIN_PREPLAY_LAST` were never seen in a header. Check them against the file.

**Three constraints apply to every experiment below.**
- **BSP and HK final tote odds are not known when the bet is placed.** A rule may *settle* at them: a BSP order placed before the off, or a tote bet. A signal *computed from* them is ex post. Each row says which.
- **Tutorial backtests assume 5% commission.** ANZ racing paid 7-10% in 2020-2026 (item 3). Re-run every result at the MBR in force for that state and date.
- **Use the back/lay midpoint, not the best back alone, at scheduled off.** The best back carries the spread, and the spread is wider on longshots. Comparing the raw best back with BSP builds a fake longshot effect into any "move" or "bias" measure.

**Measurements that stand in for the missing dictionary (all on held data):**
1. **Do the preplay fields end later than the scheduled-off snapshot?** Compare |log(preplay last / BSP)| with |log(scheduled-off midpoint / BSP)| race by race. AU races often jump after the scheduled time. If preplay last is systematically closer to BSP, the preplay window runs past scheduled off toward the actual off.
2. **Is the scheduled-off snapshot internally consistent?** Check that best back ≤ best lay, and that the back market % equals the sum of 1/best back within each race. Tabulate how often BSP falls inside the scheduled-off back/lay spread.
3. **Place terms:** count place winners per place market, and tabulate against the number of runners, non-scratched. That gives places paid by field size directly.
4. **Schema drift:** diff the column headers across the 2020-2025 zips and the 2026 monthly files, given the coverage conflict.
